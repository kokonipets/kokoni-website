How Often Should You Groom a Poodle, Teddy or Bichon?

Short answer: every 4–6 weeks for an easy-care cut, and every 4 weeks for a fluffy Asian Fusion teddy bear look.

By the Kokoni grooming team, led by Wylie Shen (12+ years of grooming experience)

Book an appointmentText or call (626) 621-4646

Poodles and teddy bear cuts

For our easy-care Simply Cute style, we recommend a groom every 4–6 weeks. For the rounder, fluffier Asian Fusion teddy bear look, come in every 4 weeks. If you keep the coat 1 inch long or longer, every 2–4 weeks works best.

Bichons

Bichons are on about the same schedule as Poodles and teddy bear cuts: every 4–6 weeks for a simple style, every 4 weeks for a fuller, rounded look.

Maltese, Schnauzers, Shih Tzus, Yorkies and Doodles

Straight-coated breeds like Maltese, Schnauzers, Shih Tzus and Yorkies also do best every 4–6 weeks. You can stretch it to 8 weeks at most; after that, the coat almost always tangles and gets dirty. Doodles are bigger dogs with a lot of coat, so we recommend every 4 weeks.

What happens if you wait too long

  • The coat starts to smell and gets dirty faster.
  • Tangles turn into mats, and heavy mats often mean the only safe option is a short shave (here's why).
  • Trapped dirt and moisture can lead to skin problems.

Brushing at home between visits

Brushing every day is ideal, and at least 3 times a week. Use a slicker brush first to open up the coat, then follow with a metal comb to make sure it runs through smoothly all the way to the skin.

Quick answer

Is it OK to wait 8 weeks?
For straight-coated breeds, 8 weeks is the maximum. For Asian Fusion styles, we recommend no more than 6 weeks, or the shape and coat become hard to maintain.
